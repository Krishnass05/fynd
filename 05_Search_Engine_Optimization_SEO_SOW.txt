STATEMENT OF WORK (SOW) — SEARCH ENGINE OPTIMIZATION SEO
Domain: Websites, IT & Software | Active Job Volume: 399 Jobs

1. Project Objective
Improve organic search visibility, keyword rankings, and domain authority through technical, on-page, and off-page SEO.

2. Scope of Work
- Resolve crawl errors, broken links, sitemaps, and core vitals.
- Identify high-intent keywords mapped to target URLs.
- Optimize meta tags, headings, image alt attributes, and schema.
- Execute white-hat backlink outreach and local citations.

3. Key Deliverables
- Technical SEO audit report and competitor gap analysis.
- Master keyword research and URL mapping spreadsheet.
- On-page implementation log and monthly ranking progress report.

4. Milestones and Payment Schedule
- M1: Technical audit and keyword research (25%)
- M2: On-page SEO implementation and technical fixes (40%)
- M3: Link building and monthly ranking report (35%)

5. Acceptance Criteria and QA
- Zero critical crawl errors in search console tools.
- High SEO audit score on standard site audit tools.
- Upward movement in search engine rankings.
