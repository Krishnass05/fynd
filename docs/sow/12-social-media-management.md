# Social Media Management

**Category:** Websites, IT and Software
**Currency:** INR
**Rate per hour:** 900
**Assumed total hours:** 30
**Deadline:** 30 working days

## Included deliverables
- Monthly content calendar with scheduled posting dates and copy (10 hours)
- Design of 15 branded static graphics and carousel slides (12 hours)
- Community engagement monitoring and monthly performance analytics report (8 hours)

## Excluded (needs a change order)
- Paid social media advertising management and ad spend optimization (15 hours)
- On-location photography or live video shoot production (18 hours)
- Influencer collaboration outreach and contract negotiation (12 hours)

## Common change requests and typical extra hours
| Request a client might make | Typical extra hours |
| --- | --- |
| Create 4 short-form video reels with voiceover and trending audio | 8 |
| Manage emergency PR communications or brand crisis response | 10 |
| Launch viral contest or giveaway campaign with compliance rules | unknown |

## Assumptions
- Client provides brand visual guidelines and product images.
- Scope covers up to 2 social channels e.g. LinkedIn and Instagram.
- Monthly content calendar approved one week prior to publishing.
