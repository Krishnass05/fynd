STATEMENT OF WORK (SOW) — DIGITAL MARKETING
Domain: Websites, IT & Software | Active Job Volume: 400 Jobs

1. Project Objective
Plan, execute, track, and optimize digital marketing campaigns to drive website traffic, lead generation, and measurable return on ad spend.

2. Scope of Work
- Conduct competitor analysis and marketing funnel planning.
- Set up and manage paid ad campaigns across Google and Meta.
- Configure conversion tracking tools, pixels, and analytics.
- Run structured A/B tests on ad headlines, visuals, and audiences.

3. Key Deliverables
- Comprehensive digital marketing strategy and media plan.
- Configured ad accounts with active campaign structures.
- Verified conversion tracking setup and performance reports.

4. Milestones and Payment Schedule
- M1: Account audit and tracking setup (25%)
- M2: Campaign launch and initial audience testing (35%)
- M3: Optimization, A/B testing, and ROI reporting (40%)

5. Acceptance Criteria and QA
- Accuracy in conversion event firing across platforms.
- Achievement of target KPI benchmarks defined in media plan.
- Timely delivery of spend and conversion reports.
