STATEMENT OF WORK (SOW) — DATA MANAGEMENT
Domain: Websites, IT & Software | Active Job Volume: 242 Jobs

1. Project Objective
Cleanse, standardize, validate, migrate, and govern business datasets across spreadsheets, databases, and CRM platforms.

2. Scope of Work
- Identify and remove duplicate records and fix corrupted entries.
- Extract, transform, and load datasets across systems.
- Establish data dictionaries and automated validation rules.
- Implement secure data archiving and access permissions.

3. Key Deliverables
- Cleaned and normalized master datasets in CSV or SQL format.
- Field mapping specification and data dictionary documentation.
- Post-migration reconciliation and validation audit report.

4. Milestones and Payment Schedule
- M1: Data audit and field mapping specification (25%)
- M2: Data cleansing, deduplication, and test migration (45%)
- M3: Full data load and documentation handover (30%)

5. Acceptance Criteria and QA
- Zero data loss or field truncation during migration.
- High accuracy rate verified via random auditing.
- Complete elimination of duplicate records.
