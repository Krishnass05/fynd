STATEMENT OF WORK (SOW) — ADOBE PREMIERE PRO VIDEO EDITING
Domain: Websites, IT & Software | Active Job Volume: 248 Jobs

1. Project Objective
Transform raw video footage and audio into high-retention video assets for YouTube, social media, or corporate campaigns.

2. Scope of Work
- Ingest raw footage, remove dead air, and establish pacing.
- Apply color correction, grading, zoom cuts, and B-roll overlays.
- Integrate animated lower thirds, titles, and captions.
- Clean audio noise and mix music and sound effects.

3. Key Deliverables
- Final mastered video exports in high resolution.
- Formatted aspect ratio variations for different platforms.
- Separate subtitle files and organized project archives.

4. Milestones and Payment Schedule
- M1: Footage review and rough cut assembly (30%)
- M2: Fine cut with graphics, grading, and sound design (50%)
- M3: Client revisions and high-res rendering (20%)

5. Acceptance Criteria and QA
- Zero audio clipping or rendering glitches.
- Adherence to brand typography and pacing guidelines.
- Delivery within agreed revision limits.
