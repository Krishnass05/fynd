STATEMENT OF WORK (SOW) — SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGEMENT
Domain: Websites, IT & Software | Active Job Volume: 183 Jobs

1. Project Objective
Build brand authority and audience growth across target social networks through strategic content creation.

2. Scope of Work
- Research industry trends to build a monthly calendar.
- Design branded graphics, carousels, and video reels.
- Schedule posts at peak hours across platforms.
- Monitor channels daily and engage with accounts.

3. Key Deliverables
- Monthly social media content calendar.
- Designed static posts, carousels, and edited reels.
- End-of-month analytics and growth report.

4. Milestones and Payment Schedule
- M1: Brand audit and first month calendar approval (25%)
- M2: Batch creation of visual assets and copywriting (40%)
- M3: Daily scheduling and performance reporting (35%)

5. Acceptance Criteria and QA
- On-time publishing adherence to calendar.
- Strict alignment with brand tone and identity.
- Month-over-month improvement in engagement rate.
